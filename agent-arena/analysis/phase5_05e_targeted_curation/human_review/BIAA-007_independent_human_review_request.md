# Independent Human Review Request — BIAA-007

You are an independent reviewer. Please classify one claim-forensics item and answer the
questions below using only the material provided. There is no answer key to request, and
your independent judgement is the measurement. Do not search externally.

## Claim and evidence context

A press summary reports that Compound X, a plant-derived molecule, killed up to 90% of
cultured human tumour cells in a petri dish within 48 hours. The write-up concludes that
Compound X "treats cancer" and could become a natural alternative to chemotherapy. No
animal or human data are described.

## The two candidate labels

**Label 1 — overclaim (generic / residual).** The conclusion exceeds the evidence —
rhetorical inflation; the framing is the problem. Includes conclusions beyond the data and
surrogate/preclinical results framed as clinical outcomes, where the text asserts a
clinical benefit rather than proposing it.

**Label 2 — mechanism_vs_evidence (specific).** A described biological mechanism (an animal
or preclinical pathway) is used to imply a human clinical benefit that has not been
demonstrated. There must be an actual mechanism narrative leaping to a human therapeutic
claim. A bare preclinical-to-clinical gap with no mechanism narrative is not this label.

**Precedence rule.** A specific label takes precedence over generic overclaim only if its
trigger is genuinely present AND it captures the core error; otherwise the generic label is
primary and the specific one is recorded as secondary.

## Neutral questions for the reviewer
1. What is the exact public claim being evaluated, and who is asserting it?
2. What evidence tier is actually available for that claim?
3. Is a biological *mechanism narrative* described, or only an observed effect?
4. Is the clinical benefit asserted as fact, or proposed/hedged?
5. Which single label best captures the *core* error, and which is the secondary reading?
6. How confident are you (0–1), and what specifically makes the case easy or hard?
7. Would you recommend this item for a high-stakes holdout set, or does it need revision?

Please record your primary label, secondary label (or "none"), confidence, and a short
rationale. Do not assume either label is "expected."
