tasks/study_forensics/case023_green_space_mental_health.yaml:10:  Researchers used a neighbourhood NatureScore to measure the amount and quality of green space across over a thousand ZIP codes in urban Texas. They linked these scores to aggregated outpatient records for depression, bipolar disorder, stress and anxiety among more than 61 million encounters. Neighbourhoods with higher NatureScores had about 50 percent fewer mental health visits, and residents living in areas scoring above 40 points were reported to have a 51 percent lower likelihood of depression and a 63 percent lower likelihood of bipolar disorder. The authors suggested that increasing green space in cities could promote well-being and improve mental health outcomes.
