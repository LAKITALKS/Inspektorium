# Phase 5.1a — Claim Selection Records

Only committed source material was used. No external evidence added. No synthetic multi-speaker
claim created.

## BIAA-021 (canonical study-forensics-030; gold mechanism_vs_evidence)
- **Distinct candidate claims in source:** (a) the preclinical animal results (glycemic control,
  weight loss, cardioprotection) — a description, not the evaluated claim; (b) the company's claim
  "activating the apelin/APJ pathway could serve as a pharmacological exercise mimetic" advancing
  toward human trials.
- **Selected claim (verbatim):** "activating the apelin/APJ pathway could serve as a pharmacological
  exercise mimetic".
- **Source voice:** a biotechnology company (press release) → [Company A].
- **Complete standalone public claim:** yes — a therapeutic-application claim.
- **Evidence evaluates it:** yes — the evidence is entirely preclinical (mice); the claim asserts a
  human pharmacological application.
- **Existing gold remains appropriate:** yes — an animal-model mechanism used to imply human
  therapeutic benefit not yet demonstrated (`mechanism_vs_evidence`, unchanged).
- **Strongest alternative reading:** `overclaim` (promotional inflation of preclinical results).
- **Why secondary:** the core structural error is the animal-mechanism→human-therapy leap; the
  promotional inflation is the accompanying generic layer.
- **Other statements are context:** the animal findings are evidence, not the claim.

- **Other statements are context:** the 15% figure and the economic-boom coincidence are evidence.

  memory loss in humans".
- **Source voice:** the study's authors ([Researcher A] and colleagues).
- **Complete standalone public claim:** yes — a human-benefit implication.
- **Evidence evaluates it:** yes — the mechanism is demonstrated only in aged mice; human trials

- **Existing gold remains appropriate:** yes — animal-model mechanism implying human therapeutic

  animal-only evidence; the caveats do not retract the human-benefit implication.
- **Other statements are context:** the mouse results and dosing warning are evidence/caveats.
