## Case material (claim + available evidence)

A cross-sectional survey of 5,000 remote workers asked participants how many houseplants they owned and assessed their depressive symptoms with a standard questionnaire. Those with five or more houseplants reported average depression scores 30 percent lower than those with no plants. The article concludes that "bringing the outdoors in" can improve mental health and urges employers to reimburse plant purchases for home offices. No experimental design is used, and the survey authors acknowledge that plant owners also tended to have higher incomes, more natural light and more leisure time.
