## Case material (claim + available evidence)

A regional workforce development agency evaluated its six-month vocational training program by comparing employment rates among participants before and after completing the program. Prior to enrollment, 34% of participants were employed. Twelve months after program completion, the employment rate among former participants had risen to 61%. The agency concluded that the program successfully increased employment by approximately 27 percentage points and recommended expanding the program to additional districts. The program director noted that the results confirmed the effectiveness of skills-based training as an intervention for unemployment.
