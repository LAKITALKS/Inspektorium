## Case material (claim + available evidence)

Rapamycin is an immunosuppressant that inhibits the mTOR pathway. In mice it reliably extends lifespan by about ten to twenty-five percent, and these results have been replicated across multiple laboratories. Enthusiasts now take the drug off-label to slow aging. However, the first well-designed human trial reported that rapamycin was safe but failed to reduce abdominal fat, its primary goal, and only modestly increased lean muscle in women. The study did not test whether the drug slows biological aging. Experts caution that no clinical evidence yet shows rapamycin slows aging in people, and off-label use carries risks, especially for those with conditions like active cancer.
