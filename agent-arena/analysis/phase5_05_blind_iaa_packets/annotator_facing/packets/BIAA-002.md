## Case material (claim + available evidence)

A randomised controlled trial involving 1,747 adults tested whether high-dose vitamin D3 could affect COVID-19 outcomes. Supplementation did not change the severity of acute infection. When researchers looked at lingering symptoms eight weeks later, 21 percent of people taking vitamin D reported at least one ongoing symptom compared with 25 percent in the placebo group. The senior author called this difference a "promising signal," but emphasised that larger trials are needed. The study was conducted remotely and started supplementation after infection onset, limiting interpretation.
