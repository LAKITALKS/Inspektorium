## Case material (claim + available evidence)

A pharmaceutical company announced results from its mid-stage trial of a new injectable cholesterol-lowering agent. The drug reduced LDL cholesterol by 65 percent in patients with familial hypercholesterolaemia who were already on maximum statin therapy. Company executives described the results as a major breakthrough in cardiovascular protection and said the drug would transform outcomes for patients at high risk of heart attack and stroke. They announced plans for an accelerated approval pathway based on the LDL reduction data. The trial measured only lipid levels and liver safety over twelve weeks; no cardiovascular events were tracked and no outcomes trial is expected to report for at least four more years.
