## Case material (claim + available evidence)

In a randomized mid-stage/3 trial, researchers tested a monoclonal antibody that clears amyloid plaques in people with inherited Alzheimer's disease. The drug dramatically reduced amyloid deposits and improved markers of neurodegeneration in participants' brains. Despite these biomarker successes, the trial failed to show any slowing of cognitive decline. Investigators emphasised the drug's strong impact on biomarkers and proposed an open-label extension, but acknowledged that its cognitive benefits remain unproven.
