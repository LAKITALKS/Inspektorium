# Phase 5 — Validation Summary

Four distinct layers are kept separate: **taxonomy-label validation**, **packet-construction
validation**, **curator governance**, and **future performance evaluation** (not yet performed).

## Proxy/model blind validation (aggregate)
- A four-rater, three-family proxy IAA over 20 blind cases produced strong agreement (four-rater
  Fleiss' κ ≈ 0.89; three families reproduced gold on all 20 primaries; the fourth diverged only on
  three borderline cases, each as a soft/secondary reading). **This is model revalidation, not human
  IAA.**
- The perfect-gold pattern was assessed honestly (shared LM priors + possible model-assisted gold
  co-evolution) and is **not** claimed as independent human-grade validation.

## Targeted curation & dispositions
- Curator review flagged soft disagreements and source-voice/claim-isolation items; material items
  received documented dispositions. Two cases were **retired** (one is **BIAA-019**; one additional
  legacy case retired in the Phase 5 intake audit) and one case (**BIAA-007**) is
  **development-only** — flagged, excluded by default, not deleted. All are holdout-ineligible.

## BIAA-013 remediation & final revalidation
- Claim-isolation defect fixed via **v2 → v3** (versioned, originals immutable). A final **two-family
  blind revalidation** (Opus 5 + GLM 5.2) reproduced the gold label unanimously; the residual
  packet-field-list note was curator-resolved as a **non-material template inconsistency**.
  **BIAA-013_v3 requires no v4.**
- **BIAA-016_v2** approved; **no further targeted revalidation** required.

## Holdout construction
- Phase 5.1 preflight hit a **feasibility fail-stop** (correlation 3, mechanism 2 < 4). Phase 5.1a
  performed a **deterministic pool augmentation** (three approved blind packets) restoring ≥4 per
  label. Phase 5.1 was then **rerun fresh** with the original frozen seed and produced the **16-case,
  four-per-label prospective holdout**, sealed and frozen.

## Contamination & leakage controls
- Evaluator-facing material scanned clean at every stage; committed artifacts expose no source
  identity or per-HLD gold; **no holdout evaluation occurred**.
