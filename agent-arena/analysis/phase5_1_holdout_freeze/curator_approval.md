## Statements
- Identities of selected source cases remain **private**; opaque IDs and cryptographic commitments
  are **frozen**.
- Phase 6–10 must **not** inspect or use the holdout.
- Holdout evaluation requires a **separately precommitted protocol**.
- **No later result-driven replacement** is permitted; a material case defect requires explicit
  retirement and deterministic replacement.
- The **16-case size limits statistical precision** (6.25 pp resolution).
- The holdout must **not** be marketed as proof of universal superiority over every LLM or prompting
  strategy. In the correlation_vs_causation and mechanism_vs_evidence strata all four eligible cases
  are selected, so source membership may be inferable to maintainers; the mappings, ordering,
  evaluator packets, and per-HLD gold remain sealed.
