- Revalidator X used **Opus 5** (Anthropic).
- Revalidator Y used **GLM 5.2** (GLM).
- Revalidator Z used **Gemini 3.5 Flash** with **Extra Thinking** (Gemini).
- All three are **distinct model families**; inference modes and product tiers differ.
- This heterogeneity may affect confidence values or label-ranking (primary vs secondary),
  and may explain why the higher-reasoning-budget rater surfaced a packet-isolation concern
  the others did not. It does **not** justify excluding or discounting any result.

- No model is described as objectively superior based on this run.
