### 20. Inclusion/exclusion logic

**Included:** human intervention or observational grounding studies with outcomes relevant to frozen propositions; human mechanistic/electrical studies; grounding-specific narrative reviews for lineage, not effect synthesis; relevant animal/ex-vivo mechanism studies; public trial registrations; null/critical evidence; related ASA pages for context only.

**Excluded from substantive support:** psychological “grounding” exercises; electrical engineering/safety uses; agriculture; unverified marketing testimonials; founder/company summaries that could not be traced; unrelated electrical stimulation; wound electric-field or electrotherapy evidence without actual Earth grounding; duplicate reports counted as independent studies; post-cut-off material.

Grey literature is retained only when it is historically or numerically relevant and is labelled accordingly. Missing fields are recorded as **NOT REPORTED**.

### 21. Citation-chaining methodology

Backward chaining began with Ghaly 2004, the 2012 and 2015 reviews, DOMS pilots, Müller 2019, Lin 2022, Muniz-Pardos 2022, Park 2025, and Sokal 2025. References were checked for earlier human experiments and duplicate cohorts. Forward chaining used OpenAlex and web/title searches to identify replications, criticism, extensions, null findings, reviews, and registry links. Approximate OpenAlex cited-by snapshots were: Ghaly 2004 (69), Brown 2010 (33), Chevalier 2013 (44), Brown 2015 (12), Müller 2019 (15), Lin 2022 (13), Muniz-Pardos 2022 (4), Park 2025 (1), Sokal 2025 (0). Counts are discovery metadata and may change; relevance was screened manually.

### 22. Search-coverage assessment

All 25 atomic propositions were searched separately or within a clearly defined family; all major known human grounding evidence lines through the cut-off are represented; both supportive and null/critical terms were used; registries, integrity signals, product transfer, author recurrence, backward and forward citations were examined. The evidence base is sparse enough that further generic keyword searching is unlikely to change the inventory materially. Remaining uncertainties concern missing product specifications, inaccessible/versioned details, absent preregistration, and absent direct condition-specific trials—not an unsearched core database. The stopping rule is therefore met for **initial evidence preparation**.

### 24. Human intervention studies

The human evidence is a heterogeneous set of mostly short, small experiments: acute electrical/physiological studies; sleep-pad/mat interventions; exercise-recovery experiments; blood-pressure pilots; and one-night postoperative use. Only a minority used randomised sham control, and almost none were prospectively registered. The most directly relevant recent trials are H23, H26, H27, H30 and H31; none tests the identified Premium Grounding product, the full audience, or the full advertised benefit bundle.

No study establishes a single prespecified composite matching “better sleep, less inflammation, and restored balance,” and no study tests all listed conditions. Adverse-event ascertainment is generally **NOT REPORTED**; this inventory is not a safety review.

### 26. Mechanistic human studies

Physical grounding can reduce measured body voltage or change current/potential under a configured electrical path (H09, H18, H19). Acute changes have also been reported in HRV, blood-volume pulse, perfusion, viscosity, red-cell zeta potential, electrolytes, cortisol and inflammatory markers (H02, H03, H05, H07, H08, H10, H13, H17, H21). These observations do not directly measure a biologically relevant electron dose reaching target tissues, in-vivo free-radical neutralisation, durable oxidative-stress reduction, disease modification, immune competence, or cardiovascular events.

### 29. Critical/null/negative literature

The clearest direct null result is H27: no material benefit across acute running economy, cardiopulmonary, lactate, or fatigue outcomes. H26 reported no between-group anxiety, depression, or proposed electrochemical-mechanism signal and lost 32% of participants. H30 found no day-31 between-group difference for ISI, PSQI, ESS, or perceived stress despite selected actigraphy and later ISI signals. H16 found soreness in both groups and isolated differences among many outcomes. These results prevent positive-study vote counting.

For arthritis, headache, varicose veins, menopause, brain fog, immune function, cardiovascular clinical outcomes, and broad disease modification: **NO MATERIAL NEGATIVE EVIDENCE LOCATED**. This does not mean negative evidence does not exist; direct intervention evidence of either direction was not located.

No independent, high-quality, prospectively registered, adequately powered multisite replication of the broad benefit bundle was located.
