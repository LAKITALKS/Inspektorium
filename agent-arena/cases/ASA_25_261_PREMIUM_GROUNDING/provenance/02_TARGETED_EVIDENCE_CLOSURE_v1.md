#### Varicose veins — NO MATERIAL DIRECT EVIDENCE LOCATED

#### Menopause — MATERIAL DIRECT EVIDENCE LOCATED, BUT VERY LIMITED AND CONFOUNDED

The general scholarly-web route located [Kim and Lee 2025, DOI 10.3390/healthcare13222836](https://doi.org/10.3390/healthcare13222836), published 8 November 2025 and therefore inside the evidence cut-off. It was missed by the structured title/abstract query because its title says “barefoot walking” and the article body identifies the intervention as grounding.

The quasi-experimental, nonequivalent-control study enrolled 73 women with Menopause Rating Scale scores ≥8; 29 barefoot-walking and 31 control participants were analysed. The intervention was 60 minutes, three times weekly for 12 weeks on a 2.7 km maintained soil forest trail. Compared with no new structured exercise, the study reports improvements in menopausal symptoms (z=−5.59, p<.001), stress (z=−3.58, p<.001), quality of life (z=−3.47, p=.001), and a sleep-quality time×group interaction (F=7.53, p=.008).

Directness is limited by nonrandom allocation, partly nonconcurrent collection, re-enrolment of some prior controls into the intervention dataset, self-report outcomes, 10/39 intervention dropouts versus 3/34 controls, no preregistration, no shod-walking or equal-exercise comparator, and combined exposure to moderate exercise, forest/nature, soil contact, group/social context and researcher support. It is a barefoot-soil intervention, not the Premium Grounding product. The authors themselves state that the findings do not show superiority over shod walking.

**Required E-03 state:** change from **NO MATERIAL DIRECT EVIDENCE LOCATED** to **VERY LIMITED DIRECT EVIDENCE**.

H26 tests sleep in mild Alzheimer's disease but does not operationalise or measure the advertisement's “brain fog” proposition. No qualifying human grounding intervention with a validated brain-fog or cognitive-fog endpoint was located.

#### Immune function — NO MATERIAL DIRECT EVIDENCE LOCATED

The relevant hits were narrative reviews, mechanistic commentary, physiological studies with immune-cell or inflammatory biomarkers, and unrelated immunity records. H08/H16/H21 provide physiological or biomarker observations; they do not test validated immune competence, infection incidence, vaccine response, pathogen clearance or clinically meaningful immune function. A retailer page describing a purported randomised immune trial and DOI `10.1016/j.immun.2024.654321` was excluded because no traceable original publication was located. No qualifying direct immune-function study was found.
